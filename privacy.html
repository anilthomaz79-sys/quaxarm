Privacy

This page describes how Quaxarm Technologies LLP handles information you send through this website. It is not a product privacy notice for a live Testora or Aegis deployment — those sit in the client’s environment.

Who we are

The controller for this website is Quaxarm Technologies LLP. Contact: contact@quaxarm.com. Office: Kochi, Kerala, India. Solutions are delivered anywhere in the world.

What this site collects

If you use the contact form, we receive the fields you submit (name, work email, organisation, phone if you give one, industry, and your message). The form is delivered to contact@quaxarm.com by FormSubmit, a third-party form service. We use that information only to reply and to follow up on the enquiry.

If you email us directly, we see whatever you include in that email.

The on-site “Ask Quaxarm” guide runs in your browser. Questions and answers are not sent to Quaxarm servers.

This is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not run our own analytics or advertising cookies on these pages. Your browser may still request fonts from Google Fonts.

Products

Testora and Aegis are designed to be deployed in the client’s environment. Authentication uses the client’s own credentials. Quaxarm does not store client operational data or credentials from those products on this website or in a Quaxarm-hosted copy of the estate.

How long we keep it

Enquiry emails are kept as long as needed to handle the conversation and ordinary business records, then deleted or archived according to that purpose.

Your rights

You can ask what we hold about you from this site, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete an enquiry we no longer need. Write to contact@quaxarm.com. You may also have rights under the law that applies to you (including, where GDPR applies, a right to complain to a supervisory authority).

Changes

We will update this page if how the website collects information changes. Last updated: 5 September 2026.
