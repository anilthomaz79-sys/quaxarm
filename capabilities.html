What we do

Five capability areas. One accountable team.

Most programmes stall because product, infrastructure, identity, and data sit with different vendors. We carry the problem from a clear diagnosis to scaled delivery — so you are not left stitching the outcome together yourself.

Product & platform engineering

We architect and build proprietary, scalable software products for how your industry actually operates — not a generic app with your logo. You get a platform that can take load, change safely, and be owned after we leave.

  • Custom platform engineering for core journeys
  • Cloud-native architecture and API-led integration
  • Full product lifecycle: discovery, build, launch, iterate
  • Operator-ready UX for staff as well as customers

Cloud, infrastructure & reliability

Growth exposes weak foundations. We design and run the enterprise IT and cloud environments that keep products available, recoverable, and cheaper to operate than a pile of one-off servers and licences.

  • Enterprise cloud architecture and landing zones
  • High availability, backup, and application management
  • Infrastructure advisory and modernisation roadmaps
  • Secure, observable production estates

Identity, workplace & cyber

If people cannot sign in safely, nothing else matters. We specialise in the control plane of the modern workplace: who gets in, from which device, to which cloud — and how you prove it.

  • Enterprise IAM and access governance
  • Microsoft 365 and workplace modernisation
  • MDM, endpoint management, and device trust
  • Cloud security architecture and hardening

Applied AI & intelligent operations

We put machine learning and automation on the processes that already generate cost and delay — forecasting, exceptions, document-heavy work, and decision support for people who run the floor, not a slide about “AI strategy”.

  • Applied ML and data science on operational datasets
  • Intelligent automation of repetitive, rules-heavy work
  • Decision-support analytics for managers and operators
  • Practical adoption: models that staff will actually use

Digital transformation advisory

Before build starts, someone has to choose what matters. Senior architects stay on the engagement to sequence the programme: which systems to replace, which to integrate, and which risks to retire first so investment shows up in the P&L.

  • Platform and digital strategy for leadership teams
  • Target architecture and investment sequencing
  • Vendor-neutral advice across cloud and workplace
  • Operating-model design so the system still runs after go-live

Sectors we serve

Cross-industry depth means we recognise the operating pattern — peaks, partners, regulation, and physical work — and still build for your specific domain.

Education

Next-generation learning, admissions, and campus administration. We help institutions launch platforms that students will use, with identity and infrastructure that survive term-time peaks and the security bar parents and regulators now expect.

Travel & hospitality

Booking, guest experience, and property operations live or die on availability. We build and harden the systems that take demand when it arrives, and give revenue and operations a single picture of the guest.

Healthcare & life sciences

Digital health is only useful if it is safe to run. We streamline clinical and administrative workflows, with cloud, identity, and integration treated as first-class requirements — not an afterthought once the app looks finished.

Marine, logistics & supply chain

Global networks fail in the gaps between systems. We create visibility, documentation, and exception handling across ports, fleets, yards, and partners so planners act on what is happening now, not on a delayed spreadsheet.

Financial services

Customer journeys and internal operations both need strong identity and a trustworthy estate. We help institutions modernise platforms and automate operational work without weakening the control environment.

Public sector & enterprise

Large organisations accumulate Microsoft 365, cloud, and device sprawl. We bring IAM, MDM, workplace, and cloud security into a governable design — so access, devices, and data are intentional rather than inherited.

Manufacturing & operations

Plants cannot pause for a fragile IT change. We connect operational technology with enterprise systems carefully: reliable infrastructure, better data for production decisions, and platforms that supervisors will use on the floor.

Retail & consumer

Peak trading is the real test. We engineer commerce, inventory, and service platforms that stay fast under load, and apply intelligence to merchandising, fulfilment, and customer operations where it changes the number, not the dashboard.

Adjacent work we take on with the same stack includes insurance, energy and utilities, real estate, and professional services — especially where identity, cloud, and operational platforms are the constraint.
